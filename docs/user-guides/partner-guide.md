Welcome to the **Lumora Digital Marketing & Cypher Swift InfoTech Business Development Partner Network**. This guide provides an end-to-end walkthrough of how to generate verified opportunities, advance client discovery, and earn approved commissions.

## 1. Onboarding & Accreditation Flow

Before you can register leads or access commercial brochures, you must complete the **5-step Onboarding Checklist**:

```
1. Create Account (Email + Phone + Password)
       ↓
2. OTP Verification (6-digit code)
       ↓
3. Profile Setup (Title, Years of Experience, Target Industries, Languages)
       ↓
4. Accept Code of Conduct & Commercial Policy (IP-logged acceptance)
       ↓
5. Partner Training Hub (Complete mandatory Lumora, Cypher Swift & Compliance modules)
       ↓
6. Operations Review & Approval (Account activated within 24 hours)
```

## 3. Registering Prospects & Duplicate Protection

1. Tap the persistent **"+ Add Lead"** button.
2. Enter company name, website, decision-maker contact details, and need summary.
3. The platform performs an automatic duplicate check against company domain, corporate email, and phone.
4. **Duplicate Found?** The platform will protect prior partner registrations while allowing you to request an ownership review if you have active, verified correspondence with the decision-maker.

---

## 4. Scheduling Discovery Calls & Recording Outcomes

1. Book meeting via Screen 18 (Discovery Meeting). Select lead, date/time, and meeting mode (Online / In-Person).
2. Attend the call alongside internal Lumora / Cypher Swift solution architects.
3. **Crucial Step:** Within 24 hours of call completion, submit **Screen 20: Record Meeting Outcome**.
   - Confirm attendance.
   - Mark as **Qualified** or **Unqualified** (with reason).
   - Document client requirements, budget range, and agreed next steps.
   - *A meeting does not count toward performance benchmarks until the outcome is recorded.*

---

## 5. Deal Closure, Payment Evidence & Incentive Tracking

1. Once the client accepts the diagnostic or service contract, submit the bank transaction UTR or receipt via Screen 22/23.
2. Internal Finance verifies deposit against corporate bank statements.
3. Upon verification, your incentive is generated automatically in the **Append-Only Earnings Ledger (Screen 25)**.
4. Discrepancies can be contested directly using the **"Raise Dispute"** button, creating an audit ticket for Finance review.

---

## 6. Client Reviews & Feedback Compliance

- Request un-gated client reviews via Screen 28 only after engagements have been delivered.
- **Strict Compliance Notice:** Offering gift cards, discounts, or incentives for 5-star ratings is strictly prohibited. Fake reviews result in immediate partner suspension.
